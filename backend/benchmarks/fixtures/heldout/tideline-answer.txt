UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
DISTRICT OF OREGON
Case No. 3:26-cv-00640

RIVERSIDE SENSORS LLC, Plaintiff,
v.
TIDELINE LABS INC., Defendant.

ANSWER TO COMPLAINT FOR BREACH OF CONTRACT

I. RESPONSES TO ALLEGATIONS
1. Tideline admits entering purchase order RL-441 for 48 probes at the stated contract price.
2. Tideline admits a carrier presented the shipment on May 18, 2026. Tideline denies that a receiving employee signed a final acceptance certificate.
3. Tideline alleges that six probes failed the on-site calibration check and that the affected cases had broken security seals.
4. Tideline denies withholding payment for absent calibration certificates. It says payment was withheld because the six probes did not meet the salinity tolerance in Appendix B.
5. Tideline says it gave written notice of rejection on May 19, 2026 and offered to make the rejected probes available for collection.
6. Tideline admits Global Harbor Freight delivered the cases and denies allegations about how they were packed before shipment.

II. AFFIRMATIVE DEFENSES
7. Tideline asserts that the unpaid balance was contingent on final acceptance after a seven-day field trial.
8. Tideline says it obtained substitute probes from Bay River Instruments and seeks recoupment of $8,400 in cover costs.
9. Tideline asks that judgment be entered for Tideline and that Riverside recover no late charges.
