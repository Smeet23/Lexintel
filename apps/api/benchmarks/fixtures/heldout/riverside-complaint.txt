UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT
DISTRICT OF OREGON
Case No. 3:26-cv-00640

RIVERSIDE SENSORS LLC, Plaintiff,
v.
TIDELINE LABS INC., Defendant.

COMPLAINT FOR BREACH OF CONTRACT

I. THE PARTIES
1. Riverside Sensors LLC manufactures aquatic monitoring devices in Portland, Oregon.
2. Tideline Labs Inc. operates waterfront testing facilities. Tideline was responsible for accepting shipment at its Astoria site.

II. FACTS
3. Under purchase order RL-441, Riverside agreed to supply 48 conductivity probes for a fixed price of $96,000.
4. Riverside alleges it tendered the final batch at the Astoria site on May 18, 2026 and provided a signed delivery manifest.
5. Riverside alleges all units arrived in factory-sealed cases. It denies that any probe failed a factory acceptance test.
6. Riverside says Tideline refused to pay the final invoice because it claimed the calibration certificates were absent.
7. On June 3, 2026 Riverside sent a written demand and attached copies of the calibration certificates along with the manifest.

III. COUNT I
8. Riverside alleges Tideline breached the purchase order by failing to pay the outstanding balance of $24,000 within 30 days of accepting delivery.
9. Riverside pleads that Astoria’s harbor closure did not excuse payment because the probes had already been received before the closure.

IV. PRAYER FOR RELIEF
10. Riverside seeks $24,000 in unpaid principal plus contractual late charges at 1.25% per month.
11. Riverside also requests an order requiring Tideline to preserve its receiving logs and warehouse camera records for the week of May 18, 2026.
