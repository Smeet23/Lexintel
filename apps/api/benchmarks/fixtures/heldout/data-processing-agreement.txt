DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT
Effective Date: April 1, 2026
Green Quay Health Ltd (Controller) and Slate Harbor Systems Ltd (Processor)

ARTICLE I INSTRUCTIONS
1.1 The Processor shall use the patient appointment records only to operate the booking platform for the Controller.
1.2 The Processor shall not analyze patient appointment records to train its own prediction models.

ARTICLE II SECURITY
2.1 The Processor must notify the Controller of a confirmed personal data breach within eighteen hours of confirming it.
2.2 The Processor shall preserve incident logs for 120 days after notifying the Controller.
2.3 The Processor must send the initial breach notice to privacy@greenquay.example.

ARTICLE III SUBPROCESSORS
3.1 The Processor shall give the Controller 21 days' written notice before engaging a new subprocessor.
3.2 The Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds within ten business days of notice.

ARTICLE IV RETURN AND DELETION
4.1 On expiry, the Processor shall return one encrypted export in CSV format to the Controller.
4.2 After the export has been accepted, the Processor must erase all production copies within 45 calendar days.
4.3 Residual copies in backups may remain until overwritten in the normal cycle, which shall never exceed 90 days after acceptance.
