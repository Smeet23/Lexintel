## Key Findings Summary

### The Core Discovery
Lawyers think through a **6-layer cognitive process**:
1. Problem Formulation (extract actual legal issue)
2. Schema Activation (apply legal frameworks)
3. Case-Based Reasoning (find similar precedents)
4. Abductive Reasoning (inference to best explanation)
5. Narrative Construction (tell persuasive story)
6. Context Integration (factor in client goals)

Your current RAG: Does #4 partially, maybe #6
Missing from current RAG: #1, #2, #3, #5

### Critical Gaps in Current AI
1. Abstract reasoning failure
2. Formal logical reasoning weakness
3. Issue spotting/problem formulation
4. Generalization across domains
5. Hallucinations in citation
6. Context collapse

### What Makes Expert Lawyers
1. Creativity (reframing, novel arguments)
2. Empathy (understanding needs and perspectives)
3. Judgment (decisions under uncertainty)
4. Strategic thinking (trade-offs, consequences)
5. Ethical reasoning (gray areas, values)

### The Opportunity
Build cognitive support layers that help lawyers think better, rather than trying to replace their thinking.
