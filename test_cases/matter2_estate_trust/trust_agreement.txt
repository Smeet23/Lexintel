THE HARRISON FAMILY REVOCABLE LIVING TRUST

TRUST AGREEMENT

This Trust Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this 5th day of March, 2024, by and between:

GRANTOR AND INITIAL TRUSTEE:
Robert J. Harrison, residing at 1845 Lakeview Drive, Greenwich, Connecticut 06830
(hereinafter referred to as the "Grantor" and "Trustee")

SUCCESSOR TRUSTEE:
First Connecticut Trust Company, located at 200 Atlantic Street, Stamford, Connecticut 06901
(hereinafter referred to as the "Successor Trustee")

ARTICLE I — CREATION AND NAME OF TRUST

1.1 Trust Name. This trust shall be known as "The Harrison Family Revocable Living Trust" (the "Trust").

1.2 Trust Property. The Grantor hereby transfers, assigns, and conveys to the Trustee the property described in Schedule A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference (the "Trust Estate"). Additional property may be added to the Trust Estate from time to time by the Grantor or by any other person with the consent of the Trustee.

1.3 Purpose. The primary purposes of this Trust are: (a) to provide for the management and disposition of the Grantor's assets during incapacity; (b) to avoid probate upon the Grantor's death; (c) to provide for the orderly distribution of assets to the Grantor's beneficiaries; and (d) to minimize estate and generation-skipping transfer taxes to the extent permitted by law.

ARTICLE II — TRUST DURING GRANTOR'S LIFETIME

2.1 Income and Principal During Lifetime. During the Grantor's lifetime, the Trustee shall pay to or apply for the benefit of the Grantor so much of the net income and principal of the Trust Estate as the Grantor may request from time to time. Any net income not so distributed shall be accumulated and added to the principal of the Trust Estate.

2.2 Incapacity Provision. If the Grantor becomes incapacitated, as certified in writing by two licensed physicians, the Trustee (or Successor Trustee if the Grantor is serving as Trustee) shall apply so much of the income and principal of the Trust Estate as the Trustee deems necessary for the Grantor's health, maintenance, support, and comfort, taking into account the Grantor's accustomed standard of living and any other resources available to the Grantor.

2.3 Revocation and Amendment. The Grantor reserves the right to revoke, amend, or modify this Trust in whole or in part at any time during the Grantor's lifetime, provided the Grantor is not incapacitated. Any such revocation or amendment must be in writing and delivered to the Trustee.

ARTICLE III — BENEFICIARIES

3.1 Primary Beneficiaries. The primary beneficiaries of this Trust are the Grantor's three children:

(a) Elizabeth A. Harrison, born April 12, 1985, currently residing in Boston, Massachusetts;
(b) William R. Harrison, born September 3, 1988, currently residing in Chicago, Illinois;
(c) Catherine M. Harrison-Park, born January 18, 1992, currently residing in San Francisco, California.

3.2 Grandchildren. As of the date of this Agreement, the Grantor has four grandchildren:

(a) Sophia Harrison (daughter of Elizabeth), born 2012;
(b) James Harrison (son of Elizabeth), born 2015;
(c) Oliver Park (son of Catherine), born 2020;
(d) Emma Park (daughter of Catherine), born 2022.

Any grandchildren born or legally adopted after the date of this Agreement shall be included as beneficiaries under the same terms.

3.3 Charitable Beneficiary. The Harrison Family Foundation, a 501(c)(3) organization established by the Grantor in 2010 (EIN: 82-1234567), is designated as a charitable beneficiary of this Trust as described in Article V.

ARTICLE IV — DISTRIBUTION UPON GRANTOR'S DEATH

4.1 Payment of Debts and Expenses. Upon the Grantor's death, the Trustee shall first pay from the Trust Estate all legally enforceable debts of the Grantor, funeral and burial expenses, costs of administering the Trust Estate, and any estate or inheritance taxes due.

4.2 Specific Bequests. The Trustee shall distribute the following specific bequests:

(a) To Elizabeth A. Harrison: the Grantor's residence located at 1845 Lakeview Drive, Greenwich, Connecticut, together with all furnishings and personal property therein, and the sum of Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000) for maintenance and upkeep of said property;

(b) To William R. Harrison: the Grantor's investment portfolio held at Morgan Stanley (Account No. ending in 4578), currently valued at approximately Three Million Dollars ($3,000,000);

(c) To Catherine M. Harrison-Park: the Grantor's interest in Harrison Commercial Properties LLC, which owns three commercial real estate properties in Fairfield County, Connecticut, collectively valued at approximately Four Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($4,500,000);

(d) To each grandchild living at the time of the Grantor's death: the sum of Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($250,000), to be held in the Education Sub-Trust described in Article VI until the grandchild reaches age twenty-five (25).

4.3 Residuary Estate. After distribution of the specific bequests, the remainder of the Trust Estate (the "Residuary Estate") shall be divided as follows:

(a) Ten percent (10%) to the Harrison Family Foundation;
(b) The remaining ninety percent (90%) shall be divided equally among the Grantor's three children (30% each), provided they survive the Grantor by at least thirty (30) days.

4.4 Predeceased Beneficiary. If any child of the Grantor predeceases the Grantor, the deceased child's share shall pass to that child's then-living descendants, per stirpes. If a deceased child has no living descendants, the share shall be divided equally among the Grantor's surviving children.

ARTICLE V — CHARITABLE PROVISIONS

5.1 Charitable Distribution. As stated in Section 4.3(a), ten percent (10%) of the Residuary Estate shall be distributed to the Harrison Family Foundation. The Foundation shall use these funds exclusively for educational scholarships for underprivileged students in Fairfield County, Connecticut, consistent with the Foundation's stated mission.

5.2 Charitable Remainder Trust Option. In lieu of the outright distribution described in Section 5.1, the Trustee, in consultation with the Grantor's tax advisor, may establish a Charitable Remainder Unitrust ("CRUT") with an annual payout rate of five percent (5%) for the benefit of the Grantor's surviving spouse (if any) for life, with the remainder passing to the Harrison Family Foundation upon the spouse's death.

ARTICLE VI — EDUCATION SUB-TRUST FOR GRANDCHILDREN

6.1 Establishment. A separate Education Sub-Trust shall be established for each grandchild who receives a bequest under Section 4.2(d).

6.2 Permitted Distributions. The Trustee may distribute income and principal from each Education Sub-Trust for the following purposes:

(a) Tuition, fees, books, and room and board at any accredited educational institution (K-12 and higher education);
(b) Educational tutoring, test preparation, and extracurricular activities;
(c) Study abroad programs;
(d) Graduate school and professional school expenses;
(e) A one-time distribution of up to $50,000 for the purchase of a first home or to start a business, upon the grandchild reaching age twenty-one (21).

6.3 Termination. Each Education Sub-Trust shall terminate upon the earlier of: (a) the grandchild reaching age twenty-five (25); or (b) exhaustion of the Sub-Trust funds. Upon termination, any remaining funds shall be distributed outright to the grandchild.

6.4 Death of Grandchild. If a grandchild dies before the Education Sub-Trust terminates, the remaining funds shall be distributed to the deceased grandchild's siblings. If there are no surviving siblings, the funds shall be added to the Residuary Estate.

ARTICLE VII — TRUSTEE POWERS AND DUTIES

7.1 Investment Powers. The Trustee shall have full power to invest and reinvest Trust assets in any form of property, including but not limited to: stocks, bonds, mutual funds, exchange-traded funds, real estate, private equity, and alternative investments. The Trustee shall exercise the judgment and care that a prudent person would use in managing their own affairs, with due regard for the purposes and terms of this Trust.

7.2 Asset Allocation. The Trustee shall maintain a diversified investment portfolio with the following general allocation guidelines: (a) no more than sixty percent (60%) in equities; (b) at least twenty percent (20%) in fixed-income securities; (c) no more than fifteen percent (15%) in alternative investments; and (d) at least five percent (5%) in cash or cash equivalents.

7.3 Real Estate Powers. The Trustee may acquire, hold, manage, improve, lease, mortgage, or sell any real property held in the Trust Estate.

7.4 Tax Elections. The Trustee shall have authority to make any tax elections available under federal and state tax laws, including elections related to the valuation of Trust assets, the timing of income recognition, and the allocation of generation-skipping transfer tax exemption.

7.5 Compensation. The Successor Trustee (First Connecticut Trust Company) shall be entitled to receive reasonable compensation for its services as Trustee, in accordance with its published fee schedule, currently 1.0% annually on the first $5,000,000 of Trust assets and 0.75% on amounts above $5,000,000.

7.6 Annual Accounting. The Trustee shall provide an annual accounting to all current beneficiaries, setting forth all receipts, disbursements, and distributions during the accounting period, together with a statement of Trust assets and their values.

ARTICLE VIII — TAX PROVISIONS

8.1 Grantor Trust Status. During the Grantor's lifetime, this Trust is intended to be treated as a "grantor trust" under Internal Revenue Code Sections 671-679. All items of income, deduction, and credit of the Trust shall be reported on the Grantor's individual income tax returns.

8.2 Estate Tax Provisions. Upon the Grantor's death, the Trustee shall allocate the Grantor's applicable exclusion amount (currently $13,610,000 for 2024) to minimize estate taxes. The Trustee may establish a Credit Shelter Trust (also known as a Bypass Trust) to preserve the full estate tax exemption.

8.3 Generation-Skipping Transfer Tax. The Trustee shall allocate the Grantor's GST exemption to trusts for grandchildren and more remote descendants to minimize generation-skipping transfer taxes.

8.4 Estimated Trust Value. As of the date of this Agreement, the estimated total value of the Trust Estate is approximately Eighteen Million Dollars ($18,000,000), consisting of:

(a) Real estate (primary residence): $3,200,000
(b) Investment accounts (Morgan Stanley): $3,000,000
(c) Investment accounts (Goldman Sachs): $4,100,000
(d) Harrison Commercial Properties LLC membership interest: $4,500,000
(e) Life insurance proceeds (upon death): $2,000,000
(f) Cash and cash equivalents: $1,200,000

ARTICLE IX — SUCCESSOR TRUSTEE PROVISIONS

9.1 Succession. First Connecticut Trust Company shall serve as Successor Trustee upon the occurrence of any of the following: (a) the Grantor's death; (b) the Grantor's incapacity; (c) the Grantor's written resignation as Trustee.

9.2 Removal. Any beneficiary holding a vested interest in the Trust may petition the Probate Court of Fairfield County, Connecticut, for the removal of the Successor Trustee for cause, including but not limited to breach of fiduciary duty, negligence, or conflict of interest.

9.3 Bond. No bond shall be required of any Trustee serving under this Agreement.

ARTICLE X — GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Governing Law. This Trust shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Connecticut, specifically the Connecticut Uniform Trust Code (Conn. Gen. Stat. §§ 45a-499 through 45a-499vv).

10.2 Spendthrift Provision. No beneficiary shall have the power to anticipate, assign, or encumber any interest in the Trust, and no interest of any beneficiary shall be subject to the claims of creditors of such beneficiary, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

10.3 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10.4 No-Contest Clause. If any beneficiary contests the validity of this Trust or any of its provisions, such beneficiary shall forfeit their entire interest in the Trust, and their share shall be distributed as if such beneficiary had predeceased the Grantor.

10.5 Perpetuities. This Trust shall terminate no later than twenty-one (21) years after the death of the last survivor of the beneficiaries named herein who are living as of the date of this Agreement.
